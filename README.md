# Chouchou Birthday Website 💙

A private, ocean-themed birthday website made for Chouchou.

## Quick start

1. Open `index.html` in a browser.
2. Open `js/counter.js` and replace `TALKING_START` with the real date/time you started talking.
3. Open `js/reasons.js` and replace the 30 sample reasons with your own.
4. Add photos to the folders under `assets/images/`.
5. Replace the photo placeholders in `story.html` with `<img>` tags when you are ready.
6. Add your own birthday music in `assets/audio/` if you want it later.
7. When finished, upload the whole folder to a static host such as Netlify.

## Suggested private content to collect before his birthday

- Childhood photos
- Beach/diving/fish photos he sends you
- First-message screenshot
- Favorite messages/quotes
- Inside jokes
- Voice-note moments
- Your pencil drawing
- A short birthday video
- His favorite fish / favorite diving place
- One or two things he says that you secretly remember
